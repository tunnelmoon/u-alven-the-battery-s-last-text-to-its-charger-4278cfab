# The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger

make.fun bounty entry: [The Battery's Last Text to Its Charger](https://make.fun/bounty/u-alven-the-battery-s-last-text-to-its-charger-4278cfab)

One line of at most 200 characters: the phone battery's final message to the charger it waited for all day.

Entry: "I held 2% for six hours out of pure spite, just so I could die the second you opened maps. You're welcome. Goodbye."

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) validates the line against the gate and writes it to `(MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR ?? "/output")/entry.txt`, with a round-trip read-back check.
